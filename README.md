### ✦ who I am

I'm **Sayma Ferdousi Fariha** — a Software Engineering student from Bangladesh, building my way from backend specialist to well-rounded engineer.

I started where most backends live: REST APIs, databases, clean architecture. Now I'm deliberately expanding — frontend, DevOps, system design, AI integration — because I want to understand the whole picture, not just one corner of it.

Outside of code, I'm a photographer who chases light, an artist breaking through creative blocks, a reader who finishes books, and someone who tends plants and loves animals. The best engineers I know are full humans first.

> *Currently preparing for an **Erasmus Semester Exchange** — open to collaborations, conversations, and new perspectives across borders.*
