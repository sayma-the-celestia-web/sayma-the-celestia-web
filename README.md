### ✦ who I am

I'm **Sayma Ferdousi Fariha** — a Software Engineering student from Bangladesh, building from backend specialist to well-rounded engineer, one deliberate step at a time.

I started where most backends live: REST APIs, databases, clean architecture, systems that *last*. Now I'm expanding — frontend, DevOps, system design, AI — because I want to understand the whole picture, not just one corner.

I'm also a photographer who chases light, an artist chipping away at a creative block, a reader who actually finishes books, and someone who tends plants and loves animals fiercely.
